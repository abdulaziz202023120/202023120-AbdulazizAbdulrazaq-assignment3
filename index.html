    E-commerce Facility Layout

    My ISE 422 term project: arrange 19 departments around a central warehouse so that the daily movement of materials is as short as possible, while the placement rules are respected.

    • Facility layout
    • Optimisation
    • Python
    • Course project
    Method
    A flow matrix between departments, a ring layout around the warehouse, and simulated annealing in Python to search for a low flow-distance cost.
    Result so far
    All placement and boundary checks pass, and the flow-distance cost is about 33% lower than my first hand-made layout under the same flows. I do not claim it is the global optimum.
    Status
    In progress (Fall 2026).
